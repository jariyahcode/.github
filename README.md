# JariyahCode

> **"Coding for the Ummah, Accumulating Jariyah for the Hereafter."**

[Baca dalam Bahasa Indonesia](README.id.md) <!-- Tombol link ke versi Indo nanti -->

JariyahCode is an open-source, non-profit organization and community for developers, designers, product managers, and tech talents to contribute voluntarily. Our core mission is to provide a structured platform for Muslim tech talents who want to dedicate their skills to the betterment of the Muslim community (Ummah). 

Here, we transform lines of code into Sadaqah Jariyah (ceaseless charity) that flows continuously, Insya Allah.

---

## 📌 Vision & Mission

### Vision
To become the largest Islamic open-source ecosystem, bridging technology talents to build an independent, open, and impactful digital civilization for the Ummah.

### Mission
*   **Build Free Software:** Developing open applications, APIs, and digital systems that solve real-world problems for mosques, Islamic social institutions, and Muslims worldwide.
*   **Inclusive Collaboration Platform:** Providing an environment for developers of all skill levels (from junior to senior) to learn, grow, and build portfolios.
*   **Tech-Charity Education:** Cultivating awareness within the tech community that contributing to open-source software can be a form of worship and a long-term investment for the hereafter.

## 🤝 How to Contribute (Join Us!)

We believe that every contribution—no matter how small—holds immense value. You don't have to be an expert to join. We welcome:

*   💻 **Backend / Frontend / Mobile Developers** (Writing and optimizing code).
*   🎨 **UI/UX Designers** (Crafting user-friendly interfaces).
*   📝 **Technical Writers / Content Creators** (Writing documentation and educational content).
*   ⚙️ **QA Engineers / Testers** (Ensuring the software runs bug-free).

### Getting Started:
1.  **Join the Community:** Connect with our core team and other volunteers on our Official Discord server via the link below.
2.  **Find Issues:** Check the `Issues` tab in our repositories and look for the `good first issue` label if you are a first-time contributor.
3.  **Fork & Pull Request:** Fork the repository, make your changes in a new branch, and submit a Pull Request (PR).

*Detailed technical contribution guidelines can be found in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).*
